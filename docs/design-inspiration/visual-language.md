# Visual-language brief

The owner's direction includes **expressive Swiss, neo-brutalism, cyberpunk,
digital design, neo-Y2K and actual dithering**. These are independent strands that
can combine. Explore a varied portfolio rather than optimizing every study for
one style, a particular era, a font choice or a conventional SaaS layout.

Use the [catalog](../design-inspiration.md) for source IDs and evidence. All 19
selected [Web & UI Design bookmarks](web-ui.md) contribute to this vocabulary.
The owner's selections guide further curation; no source is excluded merely
because it is decorative, an identity, a poster or not a literal interface control.
Use Web, Graphic Design and the 20 [Creative Coding references](creative-coding.md)
for new explorations of the full style range.

## Expressive Swiss

Treat typesetting as illustration: colorful bold text, oversized words,
sideways placement, extreme crops, staggered lines, asymmetry and unexpected
relationships between words, images and empty space. A deliberate aesthetic text
fragment can be artwork; it need not carry a navigation task or read like body
copy. Clean Swiss fonts and legibility alone are not the visual goal.

GRA-001, GRA-006, GRA-009, GRA-010 and GRA-013 offer different approaches. WEB-008
adds a huge cropped sign-off, while WEB-012 combines spatial type and dot geometry.
Try both disciplined alignment and a deliberate break in that structure.

## Neo-brutalism

Explore exposed cells and rules, emphatic boundaries, hard contrast, bold color
blocks, unapologetic scale and direct graphic interruptions. Borders, labels,
numbered stages and actions can form a composition rather than merely contain it.

WEB-001, WEB-002 and SITE-001 contribute large type and bounded structures.
GRA-002 and GRA-004 show how modular symbols, neon and colored geometry can push
that structure beyond a plain black/white grid.

## Cyberpunk

Explore neon against dark fields, cyan/magenta light, signal imagery, fine overlays
and a tension between physical surfaces and digital layers. This can be a
luminous object, atmospheric composition, image treatment or an interface detail.

GRA-008 contributes pixel light, GRA-011 contributes digital image decomposition
and fine schematic framing, and WEB-005 contributes black/green spatial depth.
CC-005 adds a cyan/magenta ring, CC-006 electric radial light and CC-019 luminous
line/glyph trails. Study this language without inferring real telemetry, rendering
methods or interactions.

## Digital design

Explore dots, pixels, raster effects, wireframes, modular geometry, technical
marks, image decomposition and deliberately synthetic surfaces. Non-type forms
can lead the composition just as strongly as typography.

GRA-003's stepped mark, GRA-004's tiles, GRA-005's wireframe twists, WEB-007's orange
cube paths and WEB-012's overlapping dots are distinct starting points. They can
be independent graphic elements before being assigned a page or product meaning.
Creative Coding adds draped/folded point fields (CC-001, CC-007), connected loops
and gestures (CC-014, CC-015, CC-020), a triangular lattice (CC-013), and technical
UI cards, keycaps and paths (CC-012).

## Neo-Y2K

Explore chrome, reflective or translucent material, sculptural objects, optical
effects, synthetic color and display surfaces. Use this as a material and
compositional vocabulary rather than a rule about when a reference was made.

GRA-012 supplies reflective chrome and huge numerals; WEB-014 combines an icy
field, reflective cube and condensed type; WEB-015 splits type around sculptural
imagery. CC-008 and CC-009 contribute glass/refraction and modular glass glyphs;
CC-016 adds a grainy spectral chrome ring. Large material studies are as valid as
small accents.

## Dithering

Make more studies where **tone and color are represented by distributing a
limited palette across small marks or pixels**. Explore how changing that
distribution changes a shaded object, gradient, silhouette or field. Dithering
should create tonal structure, not merely decorate an otherwise flat form.

CC-003's pink/yellow/blue-gray stippled field, WEB-006's four cobalt illustrated
cells and GRA-002's thermal-looking color treatment are visual starting points. The separate purple toroidal/ASCII-titled
study offers a related digital surface; its title does not verify dithering or
character-based rendering. These previews establish appearance, not the exact
algorithm. CC-003 visibly distributes its limited palette through regular pixel
stippling, but its exact ordered/error-diffusion or other method was not verified.
New studies should record their own palette, tonal treatment and any simplification.

Keep the related treatments distinct:

- **Dithering:** spatial distribution of palette colors approximates intermediate
  tones or colors.
- **Halftone:** dot coverage, size or density suggests tone; GRA-007 is a useful
  print-texture reference.
- **ASCII:** visible character glyphs build an image. A source caption alone does
  not establish a literal character-based implementation.
- **Pixel art or pixel light:** deliberate pixel shapes and luminous silhouettes,
  as in GRA-008, can exist without tonal dithering.

Treatments can overlap, but a regular dot matrix, random speckles or scattered
radial bars do not by themselves demonstrate dithering. For an editable native
study, use a controlled tonal distribution and record any coarsening needed for
its node count. Compare different palettes and mark scales on the same original
form so the visual difference is apparent.

## Authoring and application

A study can become hero artwork, a poster-like section, a transition, a frame,
a background, an identity motif or a control. A literal UI application need not
be chosen before exploring the graphic. Try several independent directions and
combinations, including non-type geometry and material-led work.

Build source-led recreations primarily from native Framer frames, shapes, text
and named groups; retain source keys and simplifications. Keep external work,
recreations and close adaptations in **Source inspiration**. Reserve **Original
compositions** for independently composed work. Existing explicit SVG/bitmap
asset studies remain assets. Record manual handoff decisions through the
[Framer workflow](../framer-prototyping.md).

When applying a composition to the site, distinguish expressive artwork from
essential product copy and actions, and test the actual interface. This practical
check should support the visual language rather than reduce the exploration to
text, tables or standard controls.
