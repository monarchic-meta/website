## Selected references

### ART-001 — Schematic landscape

- Source account: 龍 / [@DRUGONDRAGON](https://x.com/DRUGONDRAGON/status/2102733123540979945).
- Tags: `technical-art`, `negative-space`, `schematic`, `organic`.
- Observed: A still preview combines cream negative space, dark navy vertical infrastructure, tiny schematic marks, trees and human silhouettes, and sparse orange blocks.
- Use: Explore how a complex system illustration can leave quiet space for explanation. A useful bridge between technical structure and organic forms.
- Adaptation: Create an original diagram with meaningful relationships. Decorative microdetail should not look like readable system evidence.

### ART-002 — Annotated engine render

- Source account: Praveen Kumar / [@praveenisomer](https://x.com/praveenisomer/status/2095534787138113962).
- Tags: `technical-illustration`, `cutaway`, `annotation`, `material`.
- Observed: Visible still previews show a detailed gray engine cutaway with a warm orange interior, surrounded by thin callout lines and small labels on a light field.
- Use: Explore an explanatory product illustration with one focal object and a consistent annotation system.
- Adaptation: Translate the clarity and layering to Monarchic's actual software/system concepts. The source caption's handmade/no-AI claim was not independently verified; no model or rendering method was inspected.

### ART-003 — Physical object with digital overlays

- Source account: [@sodiumdream](https://x.com/sodiumdream/status/2093798162981118023).
- Tags: `digital-art`, `overlay`, `night`, `physical-digital`.
- Observed: A still preview places a street-side machine/cabinet at night under cyan/pink illumination with fine colored interface or wireframe overlays.
- Use: Explore a cover image that makes invisible software layers feel tangible, or a restrained overlay treatment for an original illustration.
- Adaptation: Keep diagrams explaining actual behavior distinct from decorative overlays; do not adopt the artwork as a product screenshot.

### ART-004 — Complex system with a human scale cue

- Source account: beeple / [@beeple](https://x.com/beeple/status/2093911298241167481).
- Tags: `digital-art`, `complexity`, `color-trails`, `scale`.
- Observed: A still preview shows a dark, fragmented block-like mass with multicolored linear trails against blue, balanced by a tiny person and a small area of landscape.
- Use: Study how one simple scale cue and a quiet background can organize very dense digital imagery.
- Adaptation: Use the compositional principle with original forms; the full spectrum is an art reference rather than a proposed UI palette.

### ART-005 — Warm light inside a cool field

- Source account: Francisco Ribeiro / [@fraveris: The Night's Quiet Glow](https://x.com/fraveris/status/2094164761550246060).
- Tags: `palette`, `texture`, `warm-cool`, `focal-light`.
- Observed: A still preview uses thick, paint-like blue and cream textures, a warm moon/light, and a vertical reflection along a river through darker fields.
- Use: Study focal contrast and a small warm highlight within a cooler field for an editorial cover or supporting visual.
- Adaptation: Keep the tactile texture in artwork and the essential typography on a quiet, readable surface. No physical medium or original artist attribution was verified.

### ART-006 — Warm architectural rhythm

- Source account: lusso / [@luusssso](https://x.com/luusssso/status/2094215703221961110).
- Tags: `palette`, `architecture`, `repetition`, `warmth`.
- Observed: Partial still-image previews show amber/orange interiors, repeated columns, layered openings, and a curved stair or balcony against a regular architectural rhythm.
- Use: Consider warmer editorial imagery or study how repeated framing creates depth without scattered decoration.
- Adaptation: Treat the period/location suggested by the caption as unverified. This is a possible atmosphere experiment, not a replacement for the existing action palette.
