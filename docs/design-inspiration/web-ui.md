## Core structure and typography

### WEB-001 — SP-01 specification layout

- Source: Kyle Anthony Miller / [@kyleanthony](https://x.com/kyleanthony/status/2102373344519586084).
- Tags: `neo-brutalist`, `swiss`, `spec-sheet`, `grid`.
- Observed: A still image uses an enormous SP-01 masthead, black/white rectangular cells, small technical headers, a capability table, and a large orbital illustration.
- Use: Explore the contrast between an enormous typographic statement, orbital artwork and fine technical cells. This can become a poster-like hero, capability composition or a family of framed graphic elements.
- Adaptation: Recompose the scale, alignment and imagery with original Monarchic content. Technical-looking decoration can be expressive; specifications or capability claims must come from actual product facts. No live site identified.

## Digital graphics and page endings

### WEB-006 — Dither cards and character graphics

- Sources: Praveen Kumar / [@praveenisomer: Dither cards](https://x.com/praveenisomer/status/2107433191036326234), [ASCII website study](https://x.com/praveenisomer/status/2007776523109970116).
- Tags: `dither`, `digital`, `cards`, `editorial`.
- Observed: A sampled feed video frame and revisited preview show four white cells with cobalt dithered abstract/figurative graphics, small serif titles, fine boundaries, and compact black controls. A separate post-detail frame shows a purple toroidal form beside serif headings and a compact metric strip.
- Use: Build several genuinely different tonal dither studies, including shaped forms, gradients and illustration families. Explore the four-cell framing, purple sculptural form and the contrast between textured artwork, serif type and compact controls.
- Adaptation: Distribute a limited palette across tonal areas when exploring actual dithering; see the distinction from halftone, ASCII and pixel art in the [visual-language brief](visual-language.md#dithering). A title or caption does not verify a rendering algorithm. Hover behavior, full motion, performance and any relationship between the two studies remain unverified; displayed metrics are not research evidence.
