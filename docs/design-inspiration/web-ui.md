## Core structure and typography

### WEB-001 — SP-01 specification layout

- Source: Kyle Anthony Miller / [@kyleanthony](https://x.com/kyleanthony/status/2102373344519586084).
- Tags: `neo-brutalist`, `swiss`, `spec-sheet`, `grid`.
- Observed: A still image uses an enormous SP-01 masthead, black/white rectangular cells, small technical headers, a capability table, and a large orbital illustration.
- Use: A product introduction or capability page where headings, diagrams, and factual specifications share a clear grid. This is one of the strongest structural matches.
- Adaptation: Borrow the hierarchy and alignment with original content; do not inherit defense imagery, capabilities, or tiny unreadable specification type. No live site identified.

### WEB-002 — CROX engineering studio

- Source: Konrad Makowski / [@konrad_designs](https://x.com/konrad_designs/status/2104948743338676568); [SITE-001](sites.md#site-001--crox).
- Tags: `neo-brutalist`, `grid`, `industrial`, `typography`.
- Observed: Sampled GIF frames show a pale gray construction grid, large black PROCESS heading, orange rectangular actions, and numbered stages. The live desktop hero also uses heavy bordered navigation cells and oversized type.
- Use: Overall page structure, service/process sections, and clear action placement. A strong starting point for a Framer direction.
- Adaptation: Keep the compositional discipline while using Monarchic's own palette, real process, and supporting facts. The demo is a template, not evidence for its example business claims.

### WEB-003 — Meridian hero concept

- Source: Simoom / [@simoomdesign](https://x.com/simoomdesign/status/2093990724325052627).
- Tags: `technical`, `hero`, `dark`, `diagram`.
- Observed: Desktop and narrow mobile still mockups pair a compact white headline with an isometric node/cube illustration, small utility labels, dark surfaces, and a yellow-green action. Sky/landscape framing surrounds the presentation mockups.
- Use: An engineering-oriented homepage statement with one focused system illustration; compare the hierarchy across proposed viewport sizes.
- Adaptation: The surrounding landscape is presentation framing, not a verified site background. Raise the contrast of small supporting details and validate real mobile behavior separately. No live site identified.

### WEB-004 — Voxa detail study

- Source: Shivang / [@shivangshah_](https://x.com/shivangshah_/status/2098007256218714207).
- Tags: `swiss`, `construction-grid`, `typography`, `product-preview`.
- Observed: Visible still previews show an off-white hero, a left-aligned black headline, thin rules, registration crosses, hatched horizontal strips, a square black action, and a dashboard over a soft photographic field.
- Use: Explore careful alignment and subtle structural marks around product messaging and screenshots.
- Adaptation: Keep essential UI distinct from decorative construction marks. Only part of the three-image carousel was visible; no live site identified.

### WEB-005 — Mapping-OS

- Source: Kyle Anthony Miller / [@kyleanthony](https://x.com/kyleanthony/status/2096936475820450291).
- Tags: `digital`, `large-type`, `visualization`, `technical`.
- Observed: A sampled video frame shows a huge white MAPPING-OS heading above a dark green spatial/terrain visualization, fine axes, tiny labels, and sparse orange markers.
- Use: Explore a legible relationship between a large title, a central visualization, and supporting metadata.
- Adaptation: Give a Monarchic diagram a clear explanatory purpose; a decorative visualization must not resemble measured evidence. Full motion and live functionality were not reviewed.

## Digital graphics and supporting sections

### WEB-006 — Dither cards and character graphics

- Sources: Praveen Kumar / [@praveenisomer: Dither cards](https://x.com/praveenisomer/status/2107433191036326234), [ASCII website study](https://x.com/praveenisomer/status/2007776523109970116).
- Tags: `dither`, `digital`, `cards`, `editorial`.
- Observed: A sampled feed video frame presents four white cells, cobalt dithered abstract/figurative graphics, small serif titles, fine boundaries, and compact black controls. A separate post-detail video frame shows a purple character-like toroidal form on white beside serif headings and a compact metric strip.
- Use: A coherent illustration family for product capabilities or research topics.
- Adaptation: Keep headings and controls as readable text. This establishes the visual treatment, not hover behavior, rendering code, or performance. The two studies' site identity and any relationship between them remain unverified; their displayed metrics are not research evidence.

### WEB-007 — Shader workflow illustration

- Source: Oğuz Yağız Kara / [@oguzyagizkara](https://x.com/oguzyagizkara/status/2100171221493027080).
- Tags: `digital`, `workflow`, `grid`, `signal`.
- Observed: A sampled video frame shows orange raster-like cube nodes and connecting paths on a white grid beside product copy.
- Use: Explore an original workflow diagram that communicates connections between real product steps.
- Adaptation: Preserve the diagram's explanation with a static or reduced-motion version. The caption names shaders; the implementation and complete animation were not inspected. No live URL verified.

### WEB-008 — Lumen Studio footer

- Source: Kartik Bansal / [@TheKartikBansal](https://x.com/TheKartikBansal/status/2093400198538309803).
- Tags: `footer`, `large-type`, `grain`, `navigation`.
- Observed: A still preview combines a conventional multi-column link area on a dark surface with a huge cropped Lumen Studio wordmark and cream, flame-like grain artwork.
- Use: Treat the footer as a deliberate composition while keeping navigation and legal content easy to find.
- Adaptation: Create original imagery and wordmark treatment; keep texture behind nonessential space and the functional links clear. No live site identified.

### WEB-009 — NORD footer direction

- Source: QClay Design / [@qclay_design](https://x.com/qclay_design/status/2097330841068150995).
- Tags: `footer`, `grid`, `monochrome`, `illustration`.
- Observed: Sampled video frames show a light page with compact, aligned navigation columns above a wide engraved-looking mountain landscape.
- Use: Explore a quiet graphic ending that gives navigation room to breathe.
- Adaptation: The landscape is artwork, not a functional requirement. Do not assume parallax or responsive behavior from these frames. No live NORD destination verified.

### WEB-013 — Framer interactive-site roundups

- Sources: Framer / [August 30 roundup](https://x.com/framer/status/2094171717325455794), [September 12 roundup](https://x.com/framer/status/2098871721113882668).

- Destinations: The first roundup's BIONA, a New Scaffolding, Parabol Labs, and TRYBREATHING appear in [SITE-002 and SITE-005–007](sites.md). Destinations for the second roundup remain unresolved.
- Adaptation: A promotional montage does not establish the individual interactions. Follow the site index's separate inspection limits.
