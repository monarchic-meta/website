# Original logo treatments

Thirteen treatments use the existing Monarchic symbol from the [pinned logo repository](https://github.com/monarchic-meta/logo/blob/1844d35d6ffdedf2356ec929c36bb5c0454cf645/logo.svg): seven procedural material studies and six SVG motion studies. LG-05–13 use grayscale treatments; the original four retain their palettes. The treatments and choreography are new; the logo geometry is existing brand artwork.

[LogoStudy.tsx](LogoStudy.tsx) contains seven material treatments. Its synchronous vector raster and cached 1024-square signed-distance atlas derive from the [canonical SVG](assets/monarchic-logo.svg); [the helper](assets/monarchic-logo-sdf.ts) and [asset-check utility](../../../scripts/build-framer-logo-assets.mjs) record the construction. The three added modes extrude the canonical field into genuine 3D signed-distance volumes and raymarch their surfaces in WebGL. The slab repeats the same XY geometry at three separated depths. Glass follows one bounded refracted interior segment and second interface; filtered transmission and reflective grazing sidewalls stabilize the sampled field. It omits the backdrop's barcode bank from transmission and does not solve recursive internal reflections or physical caustics. The atlas and filtered normals are sampled approximations, rather than analytic Bezier surfaces. Catalog references inform the first four studies' materials, textures and composition.

[LogoDropStudy.tsx](LogoDropStudy.tsx) retains six motion treatments. The fixed circle uses the source's eight cubic pieces; the finite diagonal parallelogram retains its horizontal end caps and moves along its own axis. Two instance-unique luminance masks pair each moving carrier with its visible ink. Carrier loop repeats that path three times. Mask antialiasing differs from the canonical compound path; this is not a pixel-identical assembled raster. Registration marks, Signal fall's dot field and transient outlines are additional treatment elements. Gravity pass, Magnetic catch, Signal fall and Ratchet descent fade the carrier and cut at the cycle boundary; Return stroke reverses without fading, while Carrier loop exchanges offscreen copies without fading.

The [material manifest](logo-manifest.json) records LG-01–04 and LG-11–13; the [motion manifest](logo-drop-manifest.json) records LG-05–10. In the three new material modes, palette controls affect luminance rather than hue, including when inherited colored defaults are supplied. Relief controls extrusion thickness and the slab's plate separation; Motion span controls orientation and light movement, plus slab breathing. Motion controls include scale, travel, spring bounce, echo strength, speed and still phase. Carrier loop's positive Travel sets repeat spacing and distance per cycle (`1400 + 1000 × Travel`); zero leaves one assembled carrier.

Canvas motion defaults off, and exports and thumbnails remain still. WebGL studies share at most two contexts, including initialization, with one live Canvas study or two live Preview studies. Canvas motion uses a 1,000,000-pixel budget at 12 fps; serial stills use up to 2,000,000 pixels. The SVG clock allocates no WebGL context or image buffer and yields its one Canvas/two Preview motion slots to active WebGL studies. Neither component fetches an image or font at runtime.
