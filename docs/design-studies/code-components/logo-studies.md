# Original logo treatments

Ten treatments use the existing Monarchic symbol from the [pinned logo repository](https://github.com/monarchic-meta/logo/blob/1844d35d6ffdedf2356ec929c36bb5c0454cf645/logo.svg): four procedural material studies and six SVG motion studies with grayscale presets. The treatments and choreography are new; the logo geometry is existing brand artwork.

| ID | Study | Construction and motion | Direction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LG-01 | Pigment relief | A shallow extrusion uses warm pigment, tonal dithering and moving surface light. | CC-003, GRA-002 |
| LG-02 | Optical counterform | Fine distance contours radiate around the mark while preserving its open diagonal cut. | GRA-003, GRA-005 |
| LG-03 | Chrome cut | A beveled extrusion reflects a locally authored studio environment with restrained spectral accents. | GRA-012, CC-016 |
| LG-04 | Phosphor carrier | A layered pixel-light field carries a moving signal along the diagonal counterform. | GRA-008, CC-019 |
| LG-05 | Gravity pass | The diagonal line falls down-left along its own diagonal through the fixed circle and pauses in the assembled mark. | Direct owner motion brief |
| LG-06 | Magnetic catch | The line drops, overshoots, settles into the mark, then releases downward. | Direct owner motion brief |
| LG-07 | Signal fall | Two transient line echoes follow the falling carrier and disappear in the assembled pose. | Direct owner motion brief |
| LG-08 | Ratchet descent | Discrete advances alternate with holds, including a pause in the assembled mark. | Direct owner motion brief |
| LG-09 | Return stroke | The carrier travels both ways along its diagonal, pauses at the assembled mark and reverses at the outer limits. | Direct owner motion brief |
| LG-10 | Carrier loop | Three evenly spaced carriers form a steady train through the circle; the offscreen copies exchange at the loop boundary. | Direct owner motion brief |

[LogoStudy.tsx](LogoStudy.tsx) contains the first four treatments. Its synchronous vector raster and cached 1024-square signed-distance atlas derive from the [canonical SVG](assets/monarchic-logo.svg); [the helper](assets/monarchic-logo-sdf.ts) and [asset-check utility](../../../scripts/build-framer-logo-assets.mjs) record the construction. The sampled distance field retains the four closed source features and diagonal negative space. It is not an analytic Bezier distance; subtle grid-derived chrome sidewall striations remain at close inspection. Catalog references inform materials, textures and composition.

[LogoDropStudy.tsx](LogoDropStudy.tsx) contains six motion treatments. The fixed circle uses the source's eight cubic pieces; the finite diagonal parallelogram retains its horizontal end caps and moves along its own axis. Two instance-unique luminance masks preserve the negative cut and pair each moving carrier with its visible ink. Carrier loop repeats the same path three times, with matching per-copy mask and ink positions. Mask antialiasing differs from the canonical compound path; this is not a pixel-identical assembled raster. Registration marks, Signal fall's dot field and transient outlines are additional treatment elements. Gravity pass, Magnetic catch, Signal fall and Ratchet descent fade the carrier and cut together at the cycle boundary; Return stroke reverses without fading, while Carrier loop exchanges offscreen copies without fading.

The [material manifest](logo-manifest.json) retains the first four studies' presets, provenance and verification. The separate [motion manifest](logo-drop-manifest.json) records LG-05–10. Material controls include palette, grain, motion span and dimensional Relief; motion controls include palette, scale, travel, spring bounce, echo strength, speed and still phase. The six motion presets use equal neutral circle and line tones on light or dark gray backgrounds. Carrier loop's positive Travel sets repeat spacing and distance per cycle (`1400 + 1000 × Travel`); Travel zero leaves one assembled carrier. The default still phase is assembled in all six modes. Canvas motion defaults off, and exports and thumbnails remain still. The SVG clock uses no WebGL context or image buffer, allows one active SVG Canvas study or two active SVG Preview studies, and yields motion slots to active WebGL studies. Neither component fetches an image or font at runtime.

The [06 / Logo Studies Design page](https://framer.com/projects/monarchic-io--V4oBgJHPFpR16fflzUp7-1YmJt?node=wSe9_qdcJ) and its [draft lab](https://framer.com/projects/monarchic-io--V4oBgJHPFpR16fflzUp7-1YmJt?node=XFbUB39EK) contain ten treatments and 30 placements, with [Desktop 1200](https://framer.com/projects/monarchic-io--V4oBgJHPFpR16fflzUp7-1YmJt?node=CaR79pbR4) and [inherited Phone 390](https://framer.com/projects/monarchic-io--V4oBgJHPFpR16fflzUp7-1YmJt?node=BoGMiXsRD).

The three added profiles pass 18 focused motion/lifecycle groups, including paired-copy painting and stale-node prop transitions, with zero graphics-surface allocations. Actual Framer checks cover six new-mode motion pairs across Desktop and Phone, Conveyor Pause/Play and a fresh Canvas-motion opt-in/restoration. An independent comparison matches all seven sampled pairs to the source poses, including each mask/ink copy. Phone captions fit without horizontal overflow; the reloaded Design page shows all three new assembled stills. All 18 SVG presets retain Canvas motion off, and all 105 previous placements are preserved. After restoring No, fitting a stale offscreen Phone frame into view refreshed it without a prop edit; the subsequent full reload left all twelve lab SVG instances static.

The earlier vertical-motion, monochrome and diagonal-axis verification records remain in the motion manifest's history. The earlier eighteen colored phase/aspect snapshots were not recaptured for this extension. The material manifest retains the first four studies' separate GPU and resource evidence. Images exported from Framer have not been reviewed.

The existing four material studies and their source remain unchanged. LG-05–07 retain their presets, IDs and motion profiles. The owner accepted LG-05–07’s monochrome diagonal-motion direction and requested more variations. Feedback on LG-08–10 is pending; this does not imply approval of the prior material studies. These are manual Framer handoffs; no production-site change, publication or automatic synchronization is introduced.
