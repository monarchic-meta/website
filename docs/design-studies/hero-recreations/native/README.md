# Native hero authoring records

These JSON files retain named editable frames, text, geometry and source notes
for manual Framer authoring. They do not synchronize with Framer or production.
The first-wave HERO-002, 003, 010 and 013 recipes and their measurements/layout
remain unchanged. Their saved-state evidence belongs to the historical
[first-wave handoff](../../hero-recreation-handoff.md).

The eight additional recipes below were prepared on **2026-10-31** after
reopening their public gallery/primary pages and inspecting the selected source
captures. All remain **Source inspiration**, including approximate constructions
and explicit asset stand-ins. They add no independent originals or hybrid heroes.
The overall recreation record owns final Framer node IDs, reload checks and
whole-project preservation results; recipe/planning review alone does not prove
a Framer save or functioning interaction.

These eight recipes contain **1,055 parts**. The common authoring coordinates are
1440 × 1000; source viewport/aspect differences are adapted within that study
frame. Text is editable. Native frame dimensions are positive, keys are unique
and parent groups precede their children. The first-wave recipes are preserved
as separate records rather than regenerated by this addition.

Source comparisons and planning previews are retained outside Git under the
session artifact root `hero-recreations-2026-10-31/remaining-12/`: source captures
in `sources/`, and `{HERO-ID}-planning.{svg,png}` in `native/`. Planning SVG/PNG
files are inspection aids and are not imported as the Framer artwork. The local
planning renderer uses fallback font metrics; actual Framer font/layout checks
remain distinct. `native/source-and-recipe-review.json` binds inspected captures,
recipe hashes, construction checks and retained first-wave hashes.

The `sourceEvidence` field in each recipe records its selected capture and any
known public media URL. Source-led geometry, shortened copy, font substitution,
neutral photo/film stand-ins and omitted motion remain disclosed in `note` and
in the saved study's visible credit/limitation metadata.
