## 5. Later original-hero stage

The existing [OH-001–009](../original-heroes/handoff.md) and
[OH-010–018](../original-heroes-round-two/README.md) remain preserved. Their
existence and playback passes do not answer the owner's criticism that the
directions felt alike. Resume the agreed sequence from the
[homepage brief](../../homepage-creative-brief.md) after source recreations
establish their distinct mechanisms:

1. Compare and refine several independent **information fields** that actually
   behave as spatial/dense signal environments, **geometric identities** whose
   forms construct or transform, and **research montages** with genuinely
   editorial fragments, cuts and changes of scale.
2. Give each full hero its own regular Web page and complete interaction/story
   with an ordinary-content handoff. Related studies may share Design boards;
   runnable heroes must not be clumped into a long gallery page.
3. Review differences in grayscale, geometry, density, materials and behavior,
   including Phone. Different copy or palette on the same infographic template
   does not establish three distinct directions.
4. Then explore synthesis across the three approaches. Keep premium, bold
   Monarchic R&D identity and quiet, evidence-led confidence; preserve the
   neo-brutalist, cyberpunk, digital, neo-Y2K, dithered and expressive Swiss mix.
   Independently composed work belongs in **Original compositions**; recognizable
   source adaptations retain their HERO attribution in **Source inspiration**.

A reference closes only when its observed sequence and relevant branches are
implemented or explicitly bounded, corresponding states are close to the source,
and the current Framer runtime/persistence checks pass. Unknown evidence,
substantial omitted states and declared substitutes must not be hidden behind
an “all eighteen complete” claim.
