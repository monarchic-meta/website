## Separate sources from compositions

Use two clearly distinct sections on the same design page:

- [Source inspiration](https://framer.com/projects/monarchic-io--V4oBgJHPFpR16fflzUp7-1YmJt?node=VvoNblwjH):
  the catalog's **68 unique external URLs**: **58 X posts with image or video-poster
  previews** and **10 site/support links shown as link cards**. Keep posting
  accounts, catalog IDs, and clickable source links with these works. A separate
  **Made here / source-led adaptation** area within this section holds MB-23.
- [Original compositions](https://framer.com/projects/monarchic-io--V4oBgJHPFpR16fflzUp7-1YmJt?node=rI0h8QiJa):
  **23 independently composed studies**: 22 SVGs and the MB-17 chrome bitmap,
  each in its own named group, organized into the eight clusters below. Their
  catalog IDs identify influences rather than authorship of the cited source work.

External work belongs in **Source inspiration**. Recreations and close
adaptations of an X composition belong there too, even when Codex made the
recreation. Label them as a recreation or adaptation, retain the source credit
and link, and do not present them as our original work. **Original compositions**
is reserved for independently composed studies, including the explicitly labeled
AI bitmap studies. Moving, cropping, recoloring, tracing, or regenerating a
source composition does not make it an original composition.

The first-run handoff described all 24 MB studies as original. The current
classification supersedes that label: **23 independent studies plus one
source-led adaptation**. [MB-23 Blue nocturne](https://framer.com/projects/monarchic-io--V4oBgJHPFpR16fflzUp7-1YmJt?node=c_MbcV3E6)
is an **AI-assisted adaptation made here, influenced by ART-005**. Its warm-disk
and reflection composition follows that source closely, so it belongs in Source
inspiration. The generated bitmap is not attributed to the external artist.
Its asset file, MB identifier, and Framer group/artwork IDs remain unchanged.

## Try combinations

Select a named study in Layers, duplicate its group, and move the duplicate to
the [combination playground](https://framer.com/projects/monarchic-io--V4oBgJHPFpR16fflzUp7-1YmJt?node=hakGOoQNh) beneath the wall. Crop,
layer, resize, and compare a few elements before placing them in an existing
page prototype. Use the original-composition studies for these combinations.
Keep external work and close adaptations in Source inspiration with their
attribution; do not duplicate them into a group labeled as our original work.
Neither section nor the playground defines an approved design system or
production page.

Captions and grouping frames are native Framer layers. The 22 vector studies
are SVG nodes: their placement is editable on the canvas, and internal paths
can be changed through their SVG source. The chrome hinge and blue nocturne
are bitmap studies made with built-in image_gen. MB-17 belongs in Original
compositions and has transparent surroundings and an open center. MB-23 belongs
in the source-led adaptation area within Source inspiration. External source
previews remain separate reference layers with their own credits.

## Initial creation verification

All 22 SVGs rendered with the repository-pinned Nix librsvg. The asset audit
checked XML, viewBoxes, unique local IDs, resolved fragments, absence of external
resources/scripts, all 28 referenced catalog IDs, and PNG structure/dimensions.
MB-17 is 1111 × 1415 RGBA with actual transparency; MB-23 is 1080 × 1456 opaque
RGB. No application code or dependency changes require application tests.

At initial creation, the mixed wall and all eight clusters were visually reviewed
in the Framer editor at an overview scale and at 44% close-up scale. Terrain tape and Grain fissure
were moved down 30px after review to separate their top edges from adjacent
captions. Both bitmap fills were retained in their study groups; temporary upload
frames were removed. In that initial layout, all 18 source-image asset references,
their 18 image links, and all 19 caption links were checked against the recorded
source posts. These counts describe the first run, not the expanded source gallery.

The initial overview and eight editor captures are retained in this Codex task's
local visualization artifacts. The MCP capture transport ended before returning its
screenshots, so visual verification used the saved browser captures. The design
page and both bitmap placements were confirmed in the editor. That initial
review preceded the section separation, expanded source gallery, and MB-23
reclassification described above. It does not verify those later changes.
Nothing was published, and all seven existing web-page IDs and routes remained
intact during creation.
