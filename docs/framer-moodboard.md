# Framer moodboard — visual kitchen

Created 2026-10-22 from the [inspiration catalog](design-inspiration.md).

[Open the moodboard](https://framer.com/projects/monarchic-io--V4oBgJHPFpR16fflzUp7-1YmJt?node=uj_I7x6To)
under **Design → Moodboard / Visual kitchen** in the existing project. This is a
freeform playground for graphic elements, textures, materials and compositions
that can later be applied to pages. The six homepage directions and earlier
graphic gallery are separate, preserved prototypes.

## Sources and original compositions

Keep two distinct sections on the same design page:

- [Source inspiration](https://framer.com/projects/monarchic-io--V4oBgJHPFpR16fflzUp7-1YmJt?node=VvoNblwjH)
  contains credited source-led recreations and the comparison library. The
  current catalog has **57 stable entries and 77 external URLs**: 67 X posts and
  ten site/support destinations. The 20 new Creative Coding bookmarks include
  one additional credited-original URL under CC-002; that support URL is not a
  twenty-first bookmark.
- [Original compositions](https://framer.com/projects/monarchic-io--V4oBgJHPFpR16fflzUp7-1YmJt?node=rI0h8QiJa)
  contains **18 independently composed MB studies**: 17 SVG assets and the
  MB-17 chrome bitmap. Each has its own movable group. Their cited sources are
  influences, not a claim that we created the external reference.

External work, recreations and close adaptations belong in Source inspiration,
even when Codex built the recreation. Keep the source key, posting account,
clickable post and simplification notes with each study. Cropping, recoloring,
tracing or regenerating a source composition does not make it an original.

The [visual-language brief](design-inspiration/visual-language.md) records the
owner's complementary directions: neo-brutalism, cyberpunk, digital design,
neo-Y2K, expressive Swiss composition and actual tonal dithering. Non-type
geometry, materials and effects matter alongside type-as-image. Individual
studies can explore different directions.

## Editable source recreations

The [Recreated elements area](https://framer.com/projects/monarchic-io--V4oBgJHPFpR16fflzUp7-1YmJt?node=rHeXK4bVd)
contains RE-01–20 on 1600 × 1000 artboards: 16 source recreations and four static
Creative Coding adaptations. Tiles, lettering, wires, dot glyphs,
halftone faces, pixels and construction marks are named native layers, editable
part by part. Source identities and any source metrics stay inside the clearly
labeled recreation.

The [recipe index](design-studies/recreations/README.md) and
[RE manifest](design-studies/recreations/manifest.json) retain manual authoring
sources, exact post URLs, source keys, simplifications and saved node IDs.
The fifth row contains RE-17's three-color tonal dither, RE-18's white point
canopy, RE-19's technical keycaps and connections, and RE-20's cyan/magenta
Coronal bands. All four retain source credits and static limitations. A
still-frame approximation is not a working shader or animation.

## Curation and retained records

At the owner's request, **Art & Visual Inspiration was removed from the active
catalog and Framer source library**, including its source-led painting adaptation.
MB-19–24 were also removed from the retained asset set and board. Their old files
and credits remain recoverable through repository history. X bookmarks were not
modified. ART-001–006 are retired IDs and must not be reassigned.

The 18 retained MB assets keep their bytes, identifiers and artwork nodes. MB-05
and MB-06 retain their former source credit only as historical metadata in the
[MB manifest](design-studies/moodboard/manifest.json); this does not restore the
removed Art collection. No further pruning is requested: the owner reviewed and
approved the remaining recreations, and the other original studies stay intact.

The [MB artwork index](design-studies/moodboard/README.md) links retained assets.
The [source index](design-studies/moodboard/references.json) records current
public URLs, evidence scope and preview metadata. The
[coverage record](design-studies/moodboard/catalog-coverage.json) maps stable
entries to source URLs and Framer groups, separating earlier proof from the
current saved-state verification. A metadata import receipt alone is not proof
that Framer saved the change.

## Try combinations and hand off

Duplicate a named study into the
[combination playground](https://framer.com/projects/monarchic-io--V4oBgJHPFpR16fflzUp7-1YmJt?node=hakGOoQNh).
Crop, layer and compare elements before placing them in a page prototype. Keep
source-led duplicates credited; use independently composed MB studies for
original combinations. Neither section defines approved branding or a final
production page.

Conversion to and from site code remains manual under the
[Framer workflow](framer-prototyping.md). There is no automatic asset, token,
source or CMS synchronization. Framer changes are unpublished and are not
versioned by Jujutsu.
