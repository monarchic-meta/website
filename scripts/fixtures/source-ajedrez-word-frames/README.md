# Ajedrez comparison frames

These five reference captures come from the owner-selected **Ajedrez Film Poem**
by Julián Leiss: [source post](https://x.com/julianleiss/status/2110868325052149925).
They preserve frames 1169–1173 of the retained 24 fps recording, at 480×852.

They are fixed, comparison-only test inputs for two measured glyph-geometry
fits. They are not website assets or a transcription of the source poem. The
Framer recreation retains independently authored words. Byte hashes and the
captured-frame provenance are in
[the fixture](../source-ajedrez-measured-word-fits.json).
