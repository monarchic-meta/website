# Ajedrez comparison frames

These 23 reference captures come from the owner-selected **Ajedrez Film Poem**
by Julián Leiss: [source post](https://x.com/julianleiss/status/2110868325052149925).
They retain selected frames from 1152–1224 of the 24 fps recording, at 480×852,
including the earlier five-frame arrival comparison (1169–1173).

They are fixed, comparison-only test inputs for two measured glyph-geometry
tracks. They are not website assets or a transcription of the source poem. The
Framer recreation retains independently authored words. Byte hashes, the 144
measured placements and four unresolved placements are in
[the current fixture](../source-ajedrez-two-track-extension.json). The
[earlier fixture](../source-ajedrez-measured-word-fits.json) remains immutable.
